# Referral Program

Every account gets its own shareable referral code the moment it's created — bring a friend in with it, and you both get rewarded once they actually start using the simulator.

## Finding your code

Open [Refer & Earn](app:/referrals) to see your personal referral code and a ready-to-share link. Send it to a friend, classmate, or colleague who might want to learn DevOps hands-on.

## How the reward actually works

Signing up with your code isn't enough by itself — the reward is deliberately tied to your friend **completing onboarding** (finishing the initial account setup), not just registering. This is intentional: it means the reward reflects someone who actually started using the platform, not an abandoned signup that never goes anywhere.

The moment your referred friend completes onboarding:

- **You** get **50 points** added to your score.
- **They** also get **50 points** added to theirs, as a welcome bonus.

This only ever fires once per referred person — there's no way to farm it by referring the same account repeatedly or having someone sign up and abandon the account.

## Where it shows up

Both the points you've earned from referrals and the points your referred friends earned show up in your overall score on the [Leaderboard](app:/leaderboard). The Refer & Earn page itself shows how many people have signed up with your code and how many of those have actually completed onboarding and triggered your reward.

## Try it yourself

1. Open [Refer & Earn](app:/referrals) and copy your link.
2. Send it to someone who'd genuinely benefit from this simulator.
3. Once they finish their account setup, check the [Leaderboard](app:/leaderboard) — you should both see a 50-point bump.
