# Leaderboard

Every account has a running **score** that goes up as you use the simulator — completing scenarios, earning certificates, referring friends, and generally staying active all contribute. The Leaderboard is where you see how that score stacks up against everyone else.

## Reading the board

Open [Leaderboard](app:/leaderboard) and you'll see every user ranked by score, highest first, with your own row highlighted so you can find yourself instantly even if you're well outside the top of the list — your rank is always shown separately below the visible list if you're not already on it. Display names are used instead of email addresses, so nobody else can see your account's email just from the leaderboard.

## Two views: All Users and Workshop Cohort

The Leaderboard has two tabs:

- **All Users** — every account on the platform, ranked together.
- **Workshop Cohort** — scoped to just the people who registered for the same workshop you did (matched by the email you used to register), so a class or batch can see how they're doing against each other specifically, without the noise of the entire user base.

If you registered for a workshop with a different email than your account's, or before creating an account at all, visiting the Workshop Cohort tab automatically tries to link your registration to your account (matched by email) — so it's worth checking back on that tab even if it looked empty the first time.

## What actually moves your score

Score isn't tied to any single action — it accumulates from real activity across the simulator over time. A couple of concrete, guaranteed sources: successfully referring a friend who completes onboarding adds **50 points** to both of you (see [Referral Program](app:/referrals)), and progressing through scenarios and labs contributes as you go. There's no single "correct" way to climb the leaderboard — consistent, varied use of the simulator is what actually works.

## Try it yourself

1. Open [Leaderboard](app:/leaderboard) and find your current rank.
2. If you came here from a workshop, switch to the **Workshop Cohort** tab and see your batch specifically.
3. Refer a friend (see [Referral Program](app:/referrals)) and watch both of your scores update.
