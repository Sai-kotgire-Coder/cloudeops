# My Account & Plans

Your account settings, the modules visible in your sidebar, and your Free/Pro plan all live in one place: [My Account](app:/account).

## Choosing which modules you see

CloudOps Simulator has a lot of modules, and not everyone wants all of them cluttering the sidebar at once. From My Account, you can pick exactly which ones show up for you — the same list you chose (or accepted the defaults for) during your first-time setup. Changing your selection here updates your sidebar immediately; nothing about your actual progress or data in a hidden module is lost, it's just tucked out of view until you turn it back on.

## Profile details

Your name, phone, links (LinkedIn, Instagram), and whether you're a student or a working professional all live here too — mostly used to personalize things like your certificate names and, if you choose to make your profile public, what shows on it (see [Certificates & Quizzes](app:/certificates) for the public share-link feature).

## Security

- **Change your password** — if you signed up with email/password. Accounts created via Google Sign-In don't have one until you explicitly set one here.
- **Log out of all devices** — immediately invalidates every other active session, useful if you ever suspect your account was accessed somewhere you didn't authorize.
- **Export your data** or **delete your account** entirely, if you ever want a full copy of what's stored or want to leave.

## Free vs. Pro

Every account starts on the **Free** plan. **Pro** raises the usage limits across the simulator (things like how many applications, instances, or pipelines you can create at once) — the exact current limits and pricing are always shown on the [Pricing](app:/pricing) page rather than duplicated here, since that's the page guaranteed to be up to date. If you ever hit a Free-plan limit while using a module, the simulator will tell you exactly which limit you hit and link you straight to Pricing.

## Try it yourself

1. Open [My Account](app:/account) and check which modules are currently enabled in your sidebar.
2. Fill in your profile details if you haven't already — they're what shows up on your certificates and public profile link.
3. If you're curious what Pro unlocks, check [Pricing](app:/pricing).
