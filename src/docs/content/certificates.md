# Certificates & Quizzes

Six modules — Terraform, Ansible, Vault, kubectl, GitOps, and Monitoring — track your progress and award a real certificate once you've genuinely used them, not just clicked around. This page explains exactly how that works, since it's not just "do a thing 3 times."

## Your learning path per module

Every one of the six lab pages shows a **"Your learning path"** card: three ordered, labeled steps specific to that module (e.g. Terraform's are "apply your first resource," "iterate on your config," "handle drift"). Each step checks itself off automatically as your real progress in that module crosses it — there's nothing to click, it just reflects what you've actually done.

## Reaching the milestone unlocks a quiz

Once a module's underlying counter crosses its target (3 applies for Terraform, 3 runs for Ansible, 5 accesses for Vault, 10 kubectl commands, 3 GitOps syncs, or 2 dashboard panels + 2 alert rules for Monitoring), a banner appears on that module's page:

> 🎯 Milestone reached! Take the quiz to earn your certificate.

Clicking **Take quiz** opens 4 multiple-choice questions testing whether you actually understood the concepts, not just repeated an action. You need **3 out of 4** correct to pass. Every question — right or wrong — shows an explanation afterward, so the quiz itself is still a learning moment even if you don't pass the first time.

> There's no limit on retakes. If you fail, review the explanations and try again immediately — this is a learning tool, not a proctored exam.

## Certificates are earned by the quiz, not the counter

This is the one thing worth being explicit about: reaching the counter threshold by itself does **not** issue a certificate anymore — it only unlocks the quiz. The certificate is created the moment you pass. This means someone who clicked through a module 10 times without understanding *why* doesn't get the same certificate as someone who can explain what drift or idempotency actually means.

## Viewing, printing, and sharing your certificates

Every certificate you've earned lives on the **Certificates** page ([open it](app:/certificates)), alongside a live progress bar for every module (complete with a "Take quiz" button once you've unlocked one). Click any certificate to see it rendered as a proper landscape template with your name, the module, the date, and a unique certificate code — use **Print / Save as PDF** to get a real file you can keep or attach to a resume.

You can also generate a **public share link** from the Certificates page ("Share my profile") — anyone with that link sees a read-only page with your module progress and certificates, no login required. Perfect for a LinkedIn post or a resume link. You can revoke it at any time.

## What to learn next

The Certificates page also shows a **"What to learn next"** card, pointing you at whichever module you're closest to finishing (or, if everything you've enabled is done, something you haven't tried yet). It's a nudge, not a requirement — there's no fixed order you have to follow.

## Try it yourself

1. Open any Infrastructure Lab and check its "Your learning path" card to see exactly what's left.
2. Use that module enough to cross its threshold and watch the milestone banner appear.
3. Take the quiz — if you don't pass, read the explanations and retake it.
4. Once you pass, open [Certificates](app:/certificates), click your new certificate, and try Print / Save as PDF.
